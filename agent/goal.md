# Playthrough objective

Build a beaver settlement that survives ten drought cycles without a single
starvation death.

## Priorities, in order

1. Never run out of drinking water. Storage ahead of every drought.
2. Food security before population growth.
3. Expand only when the last two are comfortably ahead of demand.

## Style

Explain each decision in one plain sentence. The audience is watching to
understand the reasoning, not to see a speedrun.
