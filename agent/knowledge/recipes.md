# Production recipes (Folktails and shared)

Unverified, from a wiki summary. The game's own data is authoritative: call inspect_building on the building to read
its real inputs, outputs and crafting time, and trust that over this list.

## Food
- Grill: 1 potato + 0.1 log -> 4 grilled potatoes (about 0.5 h). Also grilled chestnuts and grilled spadderdock.
- Gristmill (about 60 HP): 1 wheat -> 1 wheat flour (about 0.5 h). Cattails can become cattail flour.
- Bakery: 1 flour + 0.1 log -> 5 bread (about 1 h). Maple crackers: 1 flour + 1 maple syrup -> 3 (about 1.5 h).

## Industry
- Lumber Mill (about 50 HP): 1 log -> 1 plank (about 1.3 h).
- Gear Workshop: 1 plank -> 1 gear (about 3 h).
- Centrifuge: badwater -> extract. Explosive Factory: extract -> dynamite.

## Folktails only
- Refinery, biofuel: 2 potatoes + 2 water -> 30 biofuel; 2 spadderdock + 2 water -> 25; 2 carrots + 2 water -> 5.
  Potatoes are by far the best fuel crop.
- Refinery, catalyst: 1 maple syrup + 2 sunflower seeds -> 1 catalyst (speed and work boost for bots).
- Herbalist: berries -> antidote for contaminated beavers.
