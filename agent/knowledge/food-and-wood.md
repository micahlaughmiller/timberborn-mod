# Food and wood

- Berries are the emergency food: a Gatherer Flag costs nothing and is set to Berries. Farms replace them later.
- Efficient Farmhouse: put it on bare ground right next to fertile soil so no farmable tile is wasted. Carrots mature
  fastest (about 4 days) and can be eaten raw; potatoes (about 6 days) need a Grill but roughly quadruple the food per
  harvest; wheat goes through a Gristmill then a Bakery. Farmhouses can be paused or reduced once planting is done, to
  free workers for wood and construction.
- Storage: keep raw inputs next to the buildings that use them (raw potatoes are far more compact than grilled ones),
  and finished goods close to homes.
- Trees (verify with inspect_specs): birch about 7 days for 1 log, pine about 12 days for 2 logs plus resin from a
  Tapper's Shack, oak about 30 days for 8 logs. Use birch and pine for the first logs; oak groves pay off later.
- The Forester (about 30 science; 10 logs and 7 planks in this game's data) plants trees so wood does not run out.
  Players also keep working hours at 16 to 18 early on, since there are no amenities for beavers to spend time on yet.
- Teeth wear down from logging; a Teeth Grindstone repairs them.
