# Well-being and population

- Well-being speeds work and movement and lengthens life, so it is not decoration. Beavers use amenities for their
  lowest well-being needs first, so cluster campfires, rooftop terraces, lanterns, shrubs and similar around the homes.
- Folktails breed only into empty lodge beds. Add one or two lodges at a time, and only when the water and food reserves
  are rising. A sudden population jump exhausts water and is a common way for a colony to collapse.
- Budget about 2.1 water per beaver per day from the game, and plan with a safety margin up to about 3. Reserve at
  least (population x worst-case drought days x that rate) before expanding. Read the real rate from NeedSpec.
- Occupational injuries: heavy industry needs Medical Beds. Bots remove that risk late in the game.
